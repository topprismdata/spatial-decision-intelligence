# Toward an Evidence-Gated Autonomous GIS Agent: A World-Model Framework for Facility Boundary Reconstruction from Open Geodata

## Abstract

Enterprise spatial decision engines consume geofences whose quality silently determines the quality of every downstream optimization. We argue that closing this gap requires not a better algorithm but an **agent**: a system that autonomously retrieves heterogeneous open data, generates competing boundary hypotheses, verifies its own outputs against formal evidence contracts, and abstains when the evidence is insufficient. Positioning this work within the emerging "Autonomous GIS" research agenda (Li et al. 2025; PSU GeoBase research agenda), our system operates at Data-aware (L3) and Result-aware (L4) autonomy: it discovers and ingests its own evidence sources, and it evaluates and gates its own outputs.

Three formal components define the architecture. (1) A **spatial world model** — typed entities, quality findings, decision impacts, and trusted states — through which all agents perceive and act. (2) A **dual-layer ontology** separating physical entity types (14 frozen residential-domain types) from administrative land-use classes (GB50137), with legality constraints on their combinations. (3) A **five-gate evidence-verification contract** that assigns every boundary a disposition of TRUSTED / PROVISIONAL / UNRESOLVED / REJECTED, enforcing a zero-false-merge policy. A vision-language model is integrated as a perceptual instrument at two points — structured road-role assertion (+0.700 quality gain, controlled experiment) and satellite-based boundary verification — deliberately restricted from coordinate generation.

Algorithmically, a controlled ablation on park-type parcels shows that a Duckham-style concave hull (+0.007 IoU), a distance-weighted adaptive vegetation threshold (+0.008), and road-network hard clipping (+0.016) raise mean IoU from 0.368 to 0.399 (+8.4% relative) — all in O(n log n) time, CPU-only, 200 ms per parcel cache-warm. We evaluate across three facility domains in Beijing: 30 residential compounds refined over 14 failure-driven iterations (24 actionable failures → 0, zero false-trusted), 2,814 school polygons, and 204 A-level scenic areas, yielding a compliance-filtered GB50137 classification of 46,952 parcels. We close by characterizing the **semantic ceiling of autonomous boundary reconstruction**: for administratively-defined facilities, open geodata sources encode different boundary concepts, a mismatch that no perceptual model at publicly available resolutions resolves — which is precisely why the agent's abstention (PROVISIONAL with evidence documentation) is the correct output rather than a fabricated boundary.

**Keywords:** autonomous GIS, spatial world model, geospatial ontology, vision-language models, evidence gating, boundary reconstruction, OpenStreetMap, multi-agent architecture

## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 From Pipeline to Agent

The classical geofence-production workflow is a pipeline: acquire data, run extraction, ship polygons. Each handoff is silent about quality; errors propagate into territory plans, visit routes, and coverage analyses, where they become invisible. The 2025–2026 "Autonomous GIS" research program (Li et al., 2025; ARSGIS) reframes geospatial analysis as agency: systems that *reason, retrieve, execute, and verify* across five autonomy levels — routine-aware (L1), workflow-aware (L2), data-aware (L3), result-aware (L4), and knowledge-aware (L5).

This paper presents an agent architecture operating at L3–L4. Given a facility name and approximate location, the agent:

1. **Retrieves** its own evidence (L3): autonomously querying OSM extracts, Overpass, commercial POI APIs, and freely accessible satellite tiles;
2. **Reasons** over competing geometric hypotheses generated by specialized tools;
3. **Verifies** its own outputs (L4) against a formal five-gate evidence contract; and
4. **Abstains** — assigning UNRESOLVED or PROVISIONAL with a documented evidence chain — when perception cannot establish the fact.

Abstention is not a limitation of the agent but its central safety property. A wrong boundary is irrecoverable downstream (it silently shapes every solver decision that consumes it); a missing boundary is recoverable (a human reviews it, or better data arrives). The agent's disposition vocabulary has no "publish optimistically" verb.

### 1.2 Why a World Model, Not a Model

A recurring failure of end-to-end learned extractors in production GIS is that they emit geometry without provenance: the polygon is presented as fact. Our position is that the *representation* — not the network — is the load-bearing artifact. The system's spatial world model defines typed entities (what exists), quality findings (what is wrong), decision impacts (what breaks if used), and trusted states (what a solver may safely consume). Every geometric operation is expressed as a state transition in this model, and every transition carries its evidence. Learning is deployed *within* the model (cluster hulls, classifiers, VLM assertions), never *as* the model.

### 1.3 Where Vision Belongs in the Loop

Vision-language models have recently been proposed as general geospatial reasoners (GEOBench-VLM, ICCV 2025; RSVLM-QA; MapBot's LLM+SAM+DINOv2 fusion). Our controlled experiments locate VLMs precisely within the agent loop. As **percepts of structure** — a VLM asked to assert whether a road functions as a perimeter wall or internal path — they measurably improve boundary quality (+0.700 IoU-scale gain in our road-semantics experiment, Section 6.1). As **generators of coordinates**, they fail: the spatial-localization gap documented by GEOBench confirms that language-model outputs cannot be trusted as geometry. Accordingly, every VLM contribution in this system is a *typed assertion consumed by classical geometry*, never a polygon.

### 1.4 Contributions

- **A-1 (Architecture)**: An evidence-gated autonomous-GIS agent that composes a spatial world model, a dual-layer ontology, multi-hypothesis geometric reasoning, and a five-gate verification contract into a fail-closed pipeline with full provenance.
- **A-2 (Ontology)**: A dual-layer design separating physical entity identity from administrative land-use class, with legality constraints over their combinations; this composition is what allows one agent to handle compounds, schools, hospitals, and scenic areas without architectural change.
- **A-3 (Algorithms)**: A measured ablation of a three-stage boundary-refinement pipeline (concave hull, distance-weighted vegetation threshold, road clipping) — cumulative +8.4% relative IoU, all stages O(n log n), CPU-only.
- **A-4 (VLM placement)**: Empirical grounding of where vision-language perception helps (structured assertions, qualitative verification) and where it must be barred (coordinate generation), with the three-tier evidence-priority rule that prevents perceptual proximity from overriding identity evidence.
- **A-5 (Evaluation)**: Three-domain validation in Beijing (residential, schools, scenic areas; 46,952 parcels) including a candid account of the semantic ceiling — administratively-defined boundaries cannot be recovered from physically-defined open data — and the compliance design (military-parcel exclusion, precision reduction) required to publish Chinese geospatial outputs at all.

## 2. Related Work

### 2.1 Autonomous GIS and Geospatial Agents
The Autonomous GIS agenda (Li et al. 2025, *A Research Agenda for Autonomous GIS*) defines the five-level autonomy ladder and calls for self-verifying systems with error-memory and structured evaluation (GeoAnalystBench). Multi-agent orchestrators (GeoColab, MA-GeoAI) delegate data-querying, inference, and visualization to specialist agents; state-based multi-agent frameworks model workflows as finite-state machines to reduce ambiguity (IBM 2025); code-first frameworks (GISagent/GISclaw) give agents persistent geospatial sandboxes; LLM-Find addresses autonomous data discovery. Our work instantiates this agenda in a specific, safety-critical task — boundary production for decision solvers — where the verification layer (L4) is formalized as a typed gate contract rather than ad-hoc self-checking.

### 2.2 VGI Quality
Haklay (2010) and Barron et al. (2014) quantify OSM accuracy/completeness; Senaratne et al. (2017) survey assessment methods. These works *measure* quality; our gates *act* on measurement by binding quality states to consumption rights.

### 2.3 Boundary and Footprint Extraction
Supervised extractors (ISPRS benchmarks, DeepLab/U-Net lineage) reach high accuracy on labeled imagery; foundation models (SAM/SAM-2/SAM-3, Grounded SAM) enable zero-shot masks but need GPU and hallucinate at edges; lightweight variants (MobileSAM, GeoSAM-Lite) trade accuracy for CPU feasibility. Cadastral reconstruction (Meijers et al.; CadNet) maintains planar-partition invariants. Our Open-Data-Only, CPU-only, no-training constraints place us outside all three families; we instead treat existing vectors (OSM landuse, roads, POI areas) as the perceptual substrate and spend the model budget on *verification*, not extraction.

### 2.4 Urban Functional Zones
POI-composition methods (TF-IDF block scoring; LDA topic models) and multimodal fusion networks (MFFANet: Sentinel-2 + road vectors + POI) establish the block-level classification paradigm we adopt, with interpretable rules replacing learned embeddings for auditability.

### 2.5 Evidence Reasoning
Dempster-Shafer belief functions combine heterogeneous evidence under epistemic uncertainty with explicit conflict measurement (robust fusion variants, 2026). Our current ranking is heuristic; §7 identifies DS re-formulation of the gate contract as the natural principled successor.

## 3. Spatial World Model and Ontology

### 3.1 Entities, Findings, States

The world model is a typed schema through which every agent action passes:

```python
class SpatialEntity:        # what exists
    entity_id: str
    entity_type: OntologyType        # v1 physical identity
    landuse: LandUseClass            # v2 administrative use  (§3.2)
    geometry: WKT                    # WGS-84
    evidence: list[Evidence]         # each with source, confidence, timestamp

class QualityFinding:       # what is wrong
    rule_id: str                     # versioned rule reference
    severity: {CRITICAL, WARNING, ADVISORY}
    measured: dict                   # e.g. mic_diameter_m=7.1, area_m2=45000

class TrustedSpatialState:  # what a solver may consume
    entity: SpatialEntity
    gates: list[ValidationResult]
    disposition: {TRUSTED, PROVISIONAL, UNRESOLVED, REJECTED}
    consumer_ready: dict[profile, {READY, READY_WITH_WARNING, NOT_READY}]
```

**Provenance as invariant.** Every geometry field records the provider that proposed it, the gates that validated it, and the evidence each gate consulted. A reviewer can reconstruct the full causal chain from disposition to raw tile or tag. This is what makes the L4 self-verification loop auditable rather than merely automated.

### 3.2 Dual-Layer Ontology v2

The v1 ontology is a frozen residential-domain type system (14 entity types: ResidentialEstate, ResidentialPhase, ResidentialCompound, LandParcel, Building, Road, Gate, Barrier, …). Extending it by adding Hospital, School, Park as entity types produced the combinatorial drift we avoided: the system instead separates two orthogonal layers.

- **Entity layer** (physics/identity): what the thing *is* as an object in the world.
- **Use layer** (administrative): the GB50137 land-use class (R, B1, B2, M, S, A3, A4, A5, G, AGR, U) — the classification that government land-use plans and the Chinese market for spatial data actually use.

Legality constraints bind the layers:

```python
validate_pair(entity_type, landuse, profile) -> bool
# e.g. ResidentialCompound × AGR  → illegal (farmland mislabel)
#     LandParcel        × MIL    → requires P1 government evidence (else UNRESOLVED)
```

This decomposition is what allows a single agent to process compounds, schools, hospitals, and scenic areas: a facility domain is an **EntityProfile** — a set of legal entity×use pairs, area priors, tag vocabularies, and perimeter expectations — not a code fork. The five-domain Beijing deployment (§6.4: 46,952 parcels) is one agent run over the profile set.

### 3.3 Truth Priority as an Ontological Ordering

Evidence sources are ranked by *authority over the proposition in question*:

**P1 Government record** (planning documents; unavailable to us; interface reserved) **> P2 Commercial structured API** (Amap POI type chains, business district membership) **> P3 Internet corroboration** (encyclopedic/property-site attributes) **> P4 Crowdsourced OSM** (landuse/amenity tags, geometry).

The ordering is *proposition-relative*: OSM geometry outranks Amap's point-only POI for *shape*, while Amap's type chain outranks OSM's `park` tag for *identity* (sports-park case, §5.1). The gate machinery enforces this ordering mechanically rather than by convention.

### 3.4 The Formal Invariant System

The architecture is held together not by conventions but by **mechanized
invariants** — fourteen properties, each enforced in code, each with a test
that fails if the property is violated (85 dedicated tests; full-suite
regression held at parity throughout). They organize into four layers, and
the layering is itself the decoupling claim: perception advances replace
operators (layer P), new facility domains add constraint data (layer G),
fusion strategies swap behind a stable interface (layer S) — and the
container (layer C) that makes the whole auditable never changes.

**Table 1. The invariant system.**

| Layer | ID | Invariant | Formal statement | Enforcement |
|:--|:--|:---|:---|:---|
| C | INV-1 | Transition legality | only transitions in the legality table occur | `(state, transition)` checked at append |
| C | INV-2 | Chain integrity | append-only, hash-chained event log; tamper-evident and rehydratable | per-event SHA-256 over canonical JSON with prev-hash links |
| C | INV-3 | Publish gate | `PUBLISHED` requires non-empty evidence refs and input/output digests — zero-false-publish by construction | governance transition pre-check |
| C | INV-4 | Replayability | re-execution reproduces recorded digests; wall-clock is recorded but never digested | clock injection; temporal fields excluded from hashes |
| S | INV-5 | Lattice semantics | `REJECTED < UNRESOLVED < PROVISIONAL < TRUSTED`; join = max, meet = min | rank ordering overriding inherited lexicographic comparison |
| S | INV-6 | No evidence, no upgrade | disposition rises only on new SUPPORTING evidence | snapshot-history checker |
| S | INV-7 | Refutation-gated demotion | disposition falls only on REFUTING or superseding evidence | snapshot-history checker |
| S | INV-8 | Zero-false-trust | `TRUSTED` requires full required-category coverage; every snapshot is re-derivable from its evidence prefix | prefix recomputation checker |
| S | A1–A5 | Evidence algebra laws | `combine` is a commutative-associative monoid; arbitration idempotent; 0 ≤ belief ≤ plausibility ≤ 1; supporting mass never lowers belief; conflict K ∈ [0,1] with Yager fallback at K = 1 | algebra law tests over the full case matrix |
| G | INV-9 | Spec/data separation | the gate interpreter is domain-blind: a pure walk of the constraint tree over a facts mapping | interpreter holds no thresholds |
| G | INV-10 | Fail-closed specs | unknown operators or malformed nodes raise at load time, never silently pass at evaluate time | closed operator registry, load-time validation |
| G | INV-11 | Totality | missing or null facts fail constraints; sparse facts never crash a gate | total evaluator |
| P | INV-12 | Plug-in seam | new perception registers by id with zero engine change; unknown ids fail at plan resolution | operator registry |
| P | INV-13 | Lineage append-only | refinement appends operator ids, never rewrites; the method field preserves the original generator | frozen dataclass + lineage-growing constructor |
| P | INV-14 | Veto semantics | verify operators never delete candidates; vetoed hypotheses survive with explicit reports for downstream fail-closed gates | scored-candidate veto flag |

Three consequences deserve emphasis.

**Trust is derived, never stored.** Dispositions are recomputed from the
evidence set; snapshots are re-derivable from evidence prefixes (INV-8);
and publication is a transition that can only be taken with the evidence in
hand (INV-3). A fabricated TRUSTED state is not a policy violation — it is
a failed check that raises before publish.

**Audit is replay, not log-reading.** Because wall-clock time is recorded
but never digested (INV-4) and the perception agents are pure functions of
their inputs, any historical run re-executes to the identical hash chain. A
reviewer verifying a boundary re-runs the computation and compares digests:
the provenance chain is checked by computation, not by trusting prose.

**Extension points are data, not forks.** The two plugin layers are where
all growth happens. A new facility domain is a constraint dict — a school
profile differs from the residential default in area bounds alone, and the
system accepts it with zero engine changes (demonstrated in test). A new
sensor is an operator registration. Neither touches the container or the
semantic layer, which is what makes the five-domain Beijing deployment
(§6.4) one agent over one architecture rather than five pipelines.

## 4. Agent Architecture

### 4.1 Roles and Loop

The agent loop is a state machine over world-model states:

```
   [task: (name, seed_point, profile)]
              │
     ┌────────▼─────────┐
     │  A1: Resolving    │  name normalization, alias splitting,
     │      Agent        │  campus/school/venue profile selection
     └────────┬─────────┘     ── uses: Amap API (P2), gazetteer (P3)
     ┌────────▼─────────┐
     │  A2: Boundary     │  evidence gathering: OSM face search,
     │      Reasoning    │  road/POI/building context, zoom planning
     └────────┬─────────┘     ── uses: Geofabrik (P4), Overpass (P4)
     ┌────────▼─────────┐
     │  A3: Generation   │  four hypothesis providers (§4.2),
     │      + Ranking    │  evidence-weighted selection
     └────────┬─────────┘     ── uses: VLM percepts (§4.3), satellite
     ┌────────▼─────────┐
     │  A4: Verification │  five gates (§4.4), disposition,
     │      + Readiness  │  consumer-profile decisions
     └────────┬─────────┘
              │
   [TrustedSpatialState with evidence chain]   ── or ──▶ [abstention record]
```

Each arrow is a typed state transition recorded in the provenance log. There is no path from A3's geometry to publication that bypasses A4: this is the fail-closed invariant.

### 4.2 Hypothesis Providers as Agent Tools

The generation stage (A3) is where the classical algorithms live; the architecture's claim is that they are *tools under verification*, not a pipeline:

| Provider | Percept | Computational cost |
|:---|:---|:---|
| ExistingOpenBoundary | name-fuzzy match over OSM landuse/POI-area polygons | O(M) spatial join |
| RoadBlock | buffer(8–12 m by class) → union → polygonize; seed-containing block | O(n log n) |
| BuildingCluster | DBSCAN(30 m) on footprints → concave hull (Duckham, k=0.8) | O(n log n) |
| AreaPrior | profile circle prior around seed | O(1) |

The refinement suite (concave hull + distance-weighted vegetation + road clipping; §5) executes on the selected hypothesis and is re-verified by the same gates. Implementation note: providers and refinement stages are *registered operators* in a perception algebra (INV-12..14, Table 1) — composition is data, every hypothesis carries an append-only lineage, and verification appends reports rather than deleting candidates, so fail-closed gating downstream always sees the evidence, never a silent absence.

### 4.3 The VLM as Perceptual Instrument

Two integrations, both assertion-typed:

1. **Road-role assertion (generation-time)**: satellite tiles around a compound, cropped to candidate perimeter segments, are queried for a structured judgment — *perimeter-wall*, *internal-path*, *ambiguous* — returned as typed JSON. In controlled three-arm experiments against OSM-tag-only and no-semantics baselines, the VLM-asserted road semantics improved boundary quality by +0.700 on the experiment's normalized scale (§6.1), concentrated in the cases where internal community roads masquerade as perimeter roads to tag-only methods.
2. **Boundary verification (audit-time)**: rendered tiles with the candidate polygon overlaid are submitted for PASS/FAIL-with-reason judgment (§6.3). The verdict never edits geometry directly; it sets the disposition and writes into the evidence chain, where a human reviewer inherits full context.

We deliberately exclude VLM-emitted coordinates: localization benchmarks (GEOBench-VLM) confirm current models misplace small geometric primitives, and an unverifiable coordinate would violate the provenance invariant — no gate could check it.

### 4.4 Verification Contract

| Gate | Typed invariant | On violation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| G1 Ontology | entity×use pair legal for profile (or UNRESOLVED) | REJECTED |
| G2 Geometry | validity, area bounds, MIC sliver test | REJECTED |
| G3 Evidence | ≥1 independent supporting evidence link | UNRESOLVED |
| G4 External coverage | named ∨ external-POI-backed (profile threshold) | REJECTED (mislabel class) |
| G5 Consumer readiness | per-profile geometric constraints | NOT_READY |

G4 operationalizes a data-quality finding from the residential study: 4,919/11,227 unnamed `landuse=residential` polygons carry no residential POI support within 200 m and are, overwhelmingly, farmland mislabels. The gate rejects them *as a class* — the agent has learned, and the rule is auditable.

Implementation note: the five gates are declarative constraint specifications (INV-9..11, Table 1) — thresholds, branches, and combinators are serializable data interpreted by a domain-blind engine; onboarding a facility domain is a constraint dict, not an engine fork.

## 5. Verification Algorithms and Measured Effects

### 5.1 Three-Stage Boundary Refinement

**Stage S1 — Concave hull.** Duckham-style elimination: skip-one diagonals shorter than k × longest-current-edge (k = 0.8) permit vertex removal; minimal-area-loss vertex removed first; iterate to fixpoint. Corrected a real crash class discovered during 30-parcel validation (rings ≤ 4 vertices raising LinearRing construction errors), fixed with a guarded candidate loop.

**Stage S2 — Distance-weighted vegetation threshold.** Urban green connectivity (§1) motivates a radially adaptive gate: τ_eff(p) = τ_min + (τ_max − τ_min)·d(p,c)/d_max, with τ_min = 0.01 admitting interior lawns and τ_max = 0.11 rejecting distant street-tree bands. The proxy index (G−R)/(G+R) is computed on freely served satellite tiles (≈2 m/px at z16).

**Stage S3 — Road-network hard clipping.** Differencing against 8–12 m road buffers encodes the perimeter prior: urban facility walls follow roads (MiniMax qualitative analysis confirmed the "four-sides-encircled-by-roads" pattern for all three ablation parcels).

**Table 2 — Ablation (park-type parcels, n = 3, ground truth = OSM polygon):**

| Configuration | 天坛 | 颐和园 | 圆明园 | Mean | Δ mean |
|:---|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| convex hull, uniform τ | 0.364 | 0.454 | 0.285 | 0.368 | baseline |
| + concave hull | 0.381 | 0.456 | 0.288 | 0.375 | +0.007 |
| + weighted threshold | 0.398 | 0.461 | 0.289 | 0.383 | +0.008 |
| + road clipping | 0.432 | 0.469 | 0.297 | **0.399** | +0.016 |

Every stage improves every parcel (sign-consistent across n=3, 3/3 per stage; not yet significance-tested — §7).

### 5.2 Complexity and Runtime

Measured concave-hull runtime: 2.7 ms (100 pts), 9.7 ms (1,000), 38.3 ms (5,000) — consistent with O(n log n) triangulation dominance. End-to-end per parcel: 8.0 s network-bound (25 tiles, cold cache), 200 ms compute-bound warm. The 11,227-parcel full-Beijing residential run completed in 874 s (≈13/s warm) with zero provider crashes; the refinement pipeline over all 5A/4A scenic parcels ran 293 s for 34 parcels.

### 5.3 Three-Tier Membership Priority

For point-of-inclusion inference (does POI *x* belong to facility *F*?):

- **T1 identity**: name contains facility keyword → IN, HIGH confidence. Dominates all.
- **T2 containment**: point ∈ polygon → IN, MEDIUM.
- **T3 proximity**: within ε of boundary, no T1/T2 → AMBIGUOUS, LOW.

The tier order is *derived from the ontology*, not tuned: identity evidence is about *which facility*, geometric evidence about *where*, and proximity about neither with certainty. A pure-proximity rule misclassified a named campus canteen 110 m from its own wall as AMBIGUOUS (discovered in Campus Sample 01 review); the priority repair fixed it with no threshold changes. Final: 13/14 correct, 0 mismatches, 1 honest ambiguity at a genuine edge case (a commercial-tower coffee shop 123 m outside the wall).

## 6. Evaluation

### 6.1 Residential: 14 Failure-Driven Iterations (R0–R13)

30 blind-selected compounds (6 morphology × 5 strata), 360 benchmark runs, independent gold adjudication (G1–G8 protocol; zero provider visibility during golding). Actionable failure domains at baseline: 24. Each iteration targeted one domain with a typed contract rather than a code patch:

| Domain | n | Resolution mechanism |
|:---|:-:|:---|
| Road semantics | 6 | VLM perimeter assertion (§4.3-1), +0.700 |
| Building membership | 6 | function classifier, morphology-aware thresholds |
| Shared topology | 5 | evidence-conditional repair |
| Entity hierarchy | 4 | Estate→Phase→Compound resolver + gazetteer containment |
| Generation coverage | 4 | full-extract ingestion |
| Observation ceiling | 5 | abstention (by design) |

Terminal state: **24 → 0 actionable failures, false-trusted rate 0**.

### 6.2 Schools: Coverage as a Dataset Finding

2,911 education polygons (2,814 named; 1,929 schools, 646 kindergartens, 174 universities, 158 colleges). The coverage asymmetry is itself a datum: schools are densely polygonized by the OSM community precisely because they are *physically* salient, unlike administratively-defined scenic areas (§6.3) — supporting the semantic-ceiling thesis.

### 6.3 Scenic Areas: Where the Ceiling Is

204-entry A-level roster → 180 geocoded (88.2%) → 66 OSM face matches (32.4%) → 114 refined/constructed → 24 unrecoverable. Three independent metrics, deliberately separated (an early review conflated them): geocoding coverage (position found) 88.2%; geometric accuracy against OSM mean IoU 0.399; visual QC pass (MiniMax, 20 sampled) **0/20**.

The 0/20 is the paper's most important negative result. Qualitative verdicts show *both* failure directions — OSM faces under-include (museum building outlined, compound wall ignored) and constructed polygons over-include (rectangular envelope crossing walls). The administratively-defined referent and the physically-observable referent diverge by *construction*: A-level designation includes buffer zones, scenic corridors, and historical extents no mapper has drawn and no 2 m satellite pixel resolves. The agent's correct behavior here is precisely the disposition it assigns: PROVISIONAL + KNOWN_ISSUE + full evidence, never TRUSTED.

### 6.4 Composite Land-Use Classification

The same agent, profile-swapped, classified Beijing at parcel scale: 46,952 GB50137 parcels (R 11,227, G 15,812, A4 6,196, AGR 4,491, M 2,968, A3 2,911, B2 1,868, B1 635, A5 424, S 199, U 221). Label hygiene corrected 16 systematic tag/identity conflicts (sports-park class the canonical case) via the versioned rule library; a 100%-pass regression gate locks each correction permanently.

### 6.5 Amap Enrichment (P2/P3 Evidence in Action)

1,691 API calls / 629 cells / 891 s named 285 of 4,919 unnamed residential tags (5.8%) — the complement of the G4 finding: 94% of unsupported unnamed tags were correctly rejected as non-residential.

## 7. Limitations and Open Problems

1. **Semantic ceiling (§6.3)**: administratively-defined boundaries are not recoverable from physically-observable open data; better resolution or better models do not close this gap; only P1 authoritative data can.
2. **Heuristic fusion**: gate/ranking logic is rule-typed but not belief-theoretic; Dempster-Shafer re-formulation would give false-trusted *bounds* instead of observed counts (planned: R14-P3).
3. **No planar invariants**: adjacent parcels may share sliver gaps/walls; cadastral-grade consistency requires planar-partition enforcement (R14-P4).
4. **Single-city, small ablation**: n=3 per ablation row; sign-consistent but untested; cross-city validation open.
5. **VLM verification is fallible**: MiniMax critiques are informative but not certified; disposition semantics treat them as advisory-only.
6. **Regulatory boundary**: the publication of Chinese geodata is itself constrained (§8); an autonomous GIS agent operating in this domain must internalize compliance as a gate, not a policy memo.

## 8. Compliance as Architecture

Chinese geodata publication imposes hard constraints that we encoded *in the pipeline*, not in the README:

- **G-COMPAT (military exclusion)**: MIL-class parcels are dropped at export (386 removed in the Beijing product) — an export-stage gate analogous to G1, since the ontology makes "military" a first-class class.
- **G-PRECISION**: coordinates truncated to 3 decimals (~100 m) before any export, below the high-precision threshold; the internal working state keeps full precision for analysis but cannot leak through the serializer.
- **Source licensing**: ODbL attribution for all vector products; satellite tiles used only for derived classification, not redistributed.

Design note: compliance gates share the disposition vocabulary, so "legally unpublishable" is one more auditable state rather than an out-of-band deletion.

## 9. Conclusion

We presented an evidence-gated autonomous-GIS agent that reconstructs facility boundaries from open geodata under hard constraints (no proprietary APIs, no training data, CPU-only, regulatory compliance). The architecture's load-bearing elements are representational rather than algorithmic: a typed world model giving every geometry a provenance; an orthogonal entity/use ontology enabling one agent across five facility classes; a verification contract whose abstention semantics absorb the semantic ceiling that perception cannot cross. Within that ceiling, measured algorithmic refinements (concave hull, adaptive vegetation weighting, road clipping) add +8.4% relative IoU per parcel at O(n log n) cost, and a three-tier identity-over-proximity rule eliminates a class of membership errors that geometric-only logic provably cannot.

The negative result is as deliberate as the positive: for A-level scenic areas, the agent *refuses to publish* — 123 parcels carry KNOWN_ISSUE dispositions with full evidence chains — and this refusal, made possible by the world model's disposition vocabulary, is exactly what makes the remaining outputs safe for downstream decision engines.
